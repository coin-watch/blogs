# coinwatch Launches: A Faster Way to Monitor Crypto Markets

**By [Chau Ngo](https://github.com/ch-m-n)**

Today, we are launching **coinwatch**, a real-time cryptocurrency monitoring dashboard built for traders, developers, and anyone who wants a clearer view of the market.

coinwatch brings live price tracking, interactive charts, technical indicators, customizable dashboard layouts, and responsive design into one focused workspace. Built with SvelteKit, the application provides a focused and accessible experience for monitoring real-time market data.

With support for multiple themes, user accounts, saved layouts, and modular widgets such as charts, tickers, and clocks, coinwatch is designed to adapt to different workflows, from quick market checks to deeper analysis.

This launch is the beginning of the coinwatch journey. We look forward to improving the platform, expanding its capabilities, and building a better way to understand crypto markets.
